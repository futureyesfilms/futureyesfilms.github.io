ABOUT

Hi, I'm Shifin.

I'm from Kerala, India, and these days I call Lindsay, Ontario home. I love telling stories with a camera, and I share them on my YouTube channel FuturEyes and on Instagram at @futureyes___.

I also write children's books, which you'll find on Amazon, I play the violin, and I'm available for photo and video shoots. I'm a Resource Drilling & Blasting graduate from Fleming College, building my career in drilling. This page puts all of it in one place.
